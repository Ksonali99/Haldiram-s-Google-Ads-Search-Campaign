## 📌 Project Details

This project focuses on developing a performance-driven Google Ads Search Campaign strategy for Haldiram's to capture high-intent customer demand across snacks, sweets, namkeen, and gifting categories. The project demonstrates how structured search campaigns and AI-driven bidding strategies can improve conversion rates, maximize ROAS, and strengthen demand capture for FMCG and food brands.

### Key Highlights

* Search campaign architecture planning
* Intent-based keyword clustering
* Funnel-based campaign structure
* Google Ads search intent targeting
* Branded and non-branded keyword strategy
* AI-powered bidding strategy planning
* ROAS and conversion-focused optimization
* Ad copywriting and ad extension strategy
* Sitelink and callout extension planning
* Mobile-first targeting strategy
* Geo-targeting for Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities
* Ad scheduling and bid adjustment planning
* Consumer search behavior analysis
* Seasonal demand and festive gifting strategy
